Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

Top1 is a browser extension that teaches people to use AI tools, with short lessons that run inside ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and GitHub. Top1 has no servers. Nothing you do in Top1 is sent to us or to anyone else.

What Top1 stores — only on your device

Using your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local):

  • Lesson progress: current lesson, completed lessons, XP and streak
  • Skill counters: how many times you used a lesson's technique on your own, and which skills you've mastered
  • Your Kit: the prompt templates from lessons you finished
  • Settings, such as whether tips are on, and when the last tip was shown

What Top1 reads on web pages

  • Chat box text on chatgpt.com, claude.ai and gemini.google.com — to notice when you send a message and to check, on your device, whether it uses a technique from a lesson. The text itself is never saved and never leaves the page. Only the counters above are kept.
  • Labels of buttons and links on those sites and github.com — to find and highlight the right button during a lesson. Nothing is saved.

Top1 only types into the chat box when you click Insert.

What Top1 does not do

  • No accounts, analytics, tracking, cookies or ads
  • No selling, sharing or transferring of your data
  • No remote code — everything Top1 runs ships inside the extension
  • No collection of your AI conversations or browsing history. Top1 only runs on the four sites above.

Deleting your data

Remove the extension from your browser. That deletes everything Top1 stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and its date will be updated before the change takes effect.
